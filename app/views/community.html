About this grant

This funding programme provides grants to local communities and economies that are disrupted by the construction of Phase One and Phase 2a of HS2 between London and Crewe.

Who can apply

  • Public Sector and Non-profit organisations
  • Eligible regions: England

How to apply

Applications must be submitted through this service before the closing date. You will need to provide evidence of eligibility and a project plan as part of your submission.
