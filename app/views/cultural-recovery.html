About this grant

Providing targeted funding and long-term financial stability for heritage sites, museums, performing arts venues, and cultural organisations across England to sustain operations, preserve collections, and protect creative sector jobs.

Who can apply

  • Non-profit cultural organisations, accredited museums, and public galleries in England
  • Independent heritage sites, theatres, and live music venues
  • Local authority-owned cultural services and registered heritage trusts

How to apply

Applications must be submitted through this online portal before the closing date. You will need to submit audited financial accounts, a 12-month business continuity plan, and evidence of public cultural impact.
