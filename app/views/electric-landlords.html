About this grant

This grant provides landlords with support towards the cost of the purchase and installation of electric vehicle chargepoints at residential properties. Landlords can receive up to 75% off the cost of purchasing and installing a chargepoint socket (capped at £350 per socket), for up to 200 residential properties per financial year.

Who can apply

  • Landlords, right to manage (RTM) companies, and residential property management companies
  • Personal / Individual, Public Sector, Non-profit, Private Sector, and Local authority applicants
  • Must own or manage residential property with off-street parking
  • Eligible regions: National, England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, North East England, North West England, South East England, South West England, Midlands

How to apply

Applications must be submitted through an OZEV-authorised installer or via the online portal before the closing date. You will need to register as a landlord with OZEV and provide proof of property ownership or management authority.
