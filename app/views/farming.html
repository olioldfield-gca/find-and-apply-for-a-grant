About this grant

Funding ambitious research and development projects that transform agricultural productivity, build climate resilience, and lower emissions across England’s farming sector.

Who can apply

  • Farmers, growers and foresters based in England
  • Agri-tech businesses and research organisations in collaboration with farmers
  • Projects must demonstrate clear benefits to productivity and environmental sustainability

How to apply

Applications must be submitted through this service before the closing date. You will need to provide a technical project proposal, financial breakdown, and evidence of collaboration.
