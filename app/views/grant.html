What this grant is for

You can apply for funding to invest in equipment, digital improvements, new premises and innovative local projects.

Who can apply

  • Businesses based in eligible rural areas
  • Organisations with a clear project plan and evidence of local benefit
  • Applicants able to provide match funding where required
