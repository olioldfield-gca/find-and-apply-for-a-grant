About this grant

Supporting local communities, non-profits, and parish councils to accelerate clean energy transitions through local solar power generation, building heat retrofits, and energy efficiency upgrades.

Who can apply

  • Community benefit societies, charities, and social enterprises
  • Town and parish councils across England and Wales
  • Local energy groups partnered with accredited installers

How to apply

Applications must be submitted online before the deadline. You will need to submit a feasibility report, projected carbon savings calculation, and community benefit plan.
