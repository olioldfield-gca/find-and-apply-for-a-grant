About this grant

Supporting rural businesses and communities to diversify and grow through capital investment in infrastructure, equipment and skills.

Who can apply

  • Registered organisations based in England
  • Charities, social enterprises, local authorities and private businesses
  • Applicants must not be in receipt of other public funding for the same activity

How to apply

Applications must be submitted through this service before the closing date. You will need to provide evidence of eligibility and a project plan as part of your submission.
