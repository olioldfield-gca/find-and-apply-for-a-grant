About this grant

Helping small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) adopt productivity-enhancing digital technologies, modernise business operations, and grow online sales through matched funding for specialist software, digital management tools, and e-commerce solutions.

Who can apply

  • Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) registered and trading in the UK
  • Businesses employing between 1 and 249 staff with a minimum 12-month active trading history
  • Businesses adopting digital tools or replacing legacy software for the first time

How to apply

Applications must be submitted through this online portal before the deadline. You will need to submit a digital implementation proposal, vendor quotes for the proposed software/systems, and recent business financial accounts.
